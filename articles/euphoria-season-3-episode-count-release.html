Euphoria Season 3: How Many Episodes, Release Date, Cast and Everything HBO Has Confirmed

Euphoria season 3 is coming to HBO. Here is everything confirmed — episode count, premiere date, returning cast, and what the new season is actually about.

By TrendPulse Staff·May 18, 2026·3 min read·👁 8,102 views
Share:

Euphoria season 3 has 10 episodes and is scheduled to premiere on HBO in Fall 2026, with a specific date expected to be announced this summer. The season was officially greenlit following the massive viewership of season 2 and returns the core cast, though with significant changes to character trajectories following the events of the season 2 finale. Zendaya returns as Rue Bennett, and HBO has confirmed the season will tackle a significant time jump.

Euphoria Season 3 — Confirmed Details
  • Episodes: 10 (up from 8 in season 2)
  • Premiere: Fall 2026 (specific date TBA)
  • Network: HBO / Max streaming
  • Creator: Sam Levinson
  • Time jump: Confirmed — several years after season 2
  • Zendaya: Returning as Rue Bennett
  • Sydney Sweeney: Status unconfirmed (conflicting reports)

What is Euphoria season 3 about?

Creator Sam Levinson has described season 3 as "a different chapter" — one that deals with the characters as adults rather than teenagers. The confirmed time jump places the events several years after season 2, meaning the high school setting that defined the first two seasons will largely be left behind. Rue's recovery — and whether it holds — is described as the emotional center of the new season. HBO has not released a full synopsis but confirmed production wrapped in early 2026 after a prolonged shutdown.

Who is returning for season 3?

Zendaya is confirmed back as Rue. Hunter Schafer is returning as Jules. Jacob Elordi (Nate Jacobs) and Maude Apatow (Lexi) have both been photographed on set. The status of Sydney Sweeney (Cassie) remains officially unconfirmed — she has an extremely busy film schedule in 2026 and conflicting reports have emerged about the extent of her role, if any, in the new season.

When and where to watch Euphoria season 3

Season 3 will air on HBO and stream simultaneously on Max (formerly HBO Max). Episodes will drop weekly, as with previous seasons, rather than all at once. If you do not have an HBO subscription, Max is available as a standalone service starting at $9.99 per month with ads or $15.99 ad-free. The specific premiere date will be announced via HBO's official social channels — follow @HBO and @euphoria on Instagram for the first confirmation.
