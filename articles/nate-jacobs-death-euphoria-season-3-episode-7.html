How Did Nate Jacobs Die in Euphoria? Season 3 Episode 7 Death Scene, Explained in Full

Yes, Nate Jacobs is dead. Jacob Elordi's character was killed in the penultimate episode of Euphoria Season 3 — buried alive by a loan shark, he was bitten by a rattlesnake before Cassie could reach him. Here is every detail of what happened.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 25, 2026·5 min read·👁 89,114
Share:

⚠ FULL SPOILERS for Euphoria Season 3, Episode 7 below.

Yes — Nate Jacobs is dead. Jacob Elordi's character was killed in the penultimate episode of Euphoria Season 3, titled "Rain or Shine," which aired May 24, 2026 on HBO. Nate was buried alive in a coffin by loan shark Naz and died from a rattlesnake bite before Cassie could rescue him. His lifeless body — discovered as Cassie removes the coffin lid — is one of the most disturbing scenes in the show's history.

Nate Jacobs Death — Key Facts
  • Episode: Season 3, Episode 7 — "Rain or Shine" (aired May 24, 2026)
  • Cause of death: Rattlesnake bite while buried alive
  • Who buried him: Naz (Jack Topalian), the loan shark Nate owed $1 million
  • Who found him: Cassie (Sydney Sweeney)
  • Actor: Jacob Elordi — his quote: "That's a cool way to go"
  • Filming note: Production used a boa constrictor with a fake rattle; real rattlesnake for other shots
  • Euphoria finale: May 31, 2026 on HBO/Max

What happened to Nate Jacobs in Season 3?

To understand Nate's death, you need to understand how Season 3 positioned him. The Nate Jacobs of Seasons 1 and 2 — controlled, menacing, terrifyingly manipulative — had been stripped down over this season into something more pathetic: a man buried in debt, making increasingly poor decisions in an attempt to live beyond his means. At the start of Season 3, Nate borrowed $1 million from a loan shark named Naz to fund a retirement community development deal he believed would make his fortune. It did not.

Naz's collection methods escalated throughout the season. First he broke into Nate and Cassie's home. Then he severed one of Nate's fingers and sent it to Cassie as a warning. By Episode 6, Naz had given Cassie a 72-hour deadline: raise the $1 million via her OnlyFans platform, or Nate dies. When Cassie shut down her account, Naz ran out of patience entirely.

How exactly did Nate die? The rattlesnake coffin scene

In Episode 7, Naz kidnapped Nate and buried him in a wooden coffin beneath his construction site. The coffin had a small air vent — a cruel suggestion of survival that ultimately became the instrument of Nate's death. Cassie, with help from Maddy and Alamo, managed to fight off one of Naz's henchmen and locate the burial site within the 72-hour window. She was close.

She was too late. When Cassie pried open the coffin lid, she did not find a grateful Nate waiting to be saved. She found his lifeless body, necrotizing from venom — and the rattlesnake that killed him, which had crawled in through the air vent. The episode's final image is Cassie collapsing in grief over Nate's body.

"That's a cool way to go. Nate is someone who's made so many mistakes and made so many dark choices. It's cool to see it all come to what it's come to." — Jacob Elordi, in a behind-the-scenes video released by HBO

How was the death scene filmed?

According to a behind-the-scenes video released by HBO after the episode aired, the production team used a boa constrictor fitted with a fake rattle for the scenes inside the coffin with Elordi, with a real rattlesnake — nicknamed "little b*tch" by the crew — used for additional close-up shots of the snake entering the vent. Elordi filmed the coffin sequences with his shoulders literally touching the sides of the box and the lid drilled shut. He described the experience as "actually quite peaceful" — a characteristically unbothered response from the actor.

Will Nate appear in the Euphoria finale?

Almost certainly not in a living capacity. The Euphoria Season 3 finale airs May 31, 2026 on HBO and Max. Sam Levinson has structured the season in a way that Nate's death in Episode 7 is designed to set the tone for a darker, more violent finale — he is described in post-episode commentary as likely not the last significant character to die this season. Whether Nate appears in flashbacks is unknown, but his storyline has conclusively ended. The finale is expected to resolve Rue's dangerous situation (she was left in extreme peril at the end of Episode 7), Cassie's grief and rage, and what happens to Faye after she screamed at the end of the episode following a confrontation over missing cartel money.

Was Nate's death the ending he deserved?

That depends entirely on how you feel about the character. Nate Jacobs spent two seasons terrorizing nearly everyone around him — blackmailing Jules, abusing Maddy, manipulating his family. By Season 3, the show had reduced him to a smaller kind of threat: financially reckless, still controlling in his relationship with Cassie, but no longer the supernatural figure of menace he once was. His death — not at the hands of any of his victims, not as a consequence of his worst acts, but from a random rattlesnake in a box because of a business deal gone wrong — is either perfectly ironic or deeply unsatisfying, depending on your appetite for narrative justice. The show leaned into the irony fully, and it will be argued about for years.
