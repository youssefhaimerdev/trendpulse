GTA 6: Release Date, Pre-Order Details, Platforms, Price, Characters — Everything Rockstar Has Confirmed

GTA 6 is finally real. Rockstar Games has confirmed the release date, platforms, price, and key gameplay details. Here is every confirmed fact in one place so you don't have to piece it together from a dozen sources.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 18, 2026·6 min read·👁 14,220 views
Share:

GTA 6 releases on May 26, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. Pre-orders are live now at major retailers and the PlayStation and Xbox digital stores at a standard price of $69.99. A PC release date has not been confirmed — Rockstar's pattern with previous GTA titles suggests a PC version will follow approximately 12 to 18 months after the console launch.

GTA 6 — Confirmed Details
  • Release date: May 26, 2026
  • Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X/S (PC TBC)
  • Price: $69.99 standard / $89.99 deluxe edition
  • Setting: Vice City (modern-day Miami-inspired)
  • Protagonists: Lucia (female lead) and Jason
  • Developer: Rockstar Games
  • Publisher: Take-Two Interactive
  • Online mode: GTA Online VI launching 3 months post-release

Where is GTA 6 set and who are the characters?

GTA 6 returns to Vice City — Rockstar's fictional version of Miami — for the first time since GTA: Vice City in 2002. The map is confirmed to be significantly larger than GTA V's Los Santos, encompassing a sprawling modern city, surrounding suburbs, Everglades-style wetlands, and smaller satellite towns. The game features two playable protagonists: Lucia, a woman with a criminal past who is the first female lead in mainline GTA history, and Jason, her partner in crime. The story centers on their relationship and a series of escalating heists across Vice City and the surrounding state of Leonida.

What gameplay features have been confirmed?

Rockstar has confirmed several major gameplay improvements over GTA V. The wanted system has been completely redesigned, with law enforcement responding more realistically to the nature and location of crimes. NPC behavior is significantly more dynamic — characters remember interactions and react to the player's in-game reputation. The game includes a fully interactive economy where prices in the world fluctuate based on in-game events. Interior spaces — stores, restaurants, homes — are far more accessible and detailed than in previous entries.

Should you pre-order GTA 6?

Pre-ordering the standard edition gets you access to the game on launch day plus a small package of in-game currency for GTA Online VI when it launches. The deluxe edition ($89.99) adds a larger currency bonus, an exclusive vehicle, and a digital art book. Neither edition offers early access — all versions unlock at the same time on May 26. If you're confident you want the game at launch, pre-ordering from a digital store guarantees a midnight unlock with no trip to a retailer required.

When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Rockstar has not confirmed a PC release date. GTA V launched on PS3/Xbox 360 in 2013 and didn't arrive on PC until 2015. Red Dead Redemption 2 followed a similar pattern with a 12-month gap. Most analysts expect GTA 6 on PC sometime in 2027, though Rockstar may announce this closer to the console launch. PC players should not expect to play the game in 2026.
