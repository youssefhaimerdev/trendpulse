Memorial Day 2026: Everything Open and Closed Today — Walmart, Costco, Target, USPS, Banks

It's Memorial Day, May 25, 2026. Here is a complete, fast-answer guide to what is open, what is closed, what time stores close, and everything else you need before heading out today.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 25, 2026·4 min read·👁 183,441
Share:

The most-searched question in America right now is simple: what's open on Memorial Day 2026? The short answer: most stores are open, banks are closed, the post office is closed, and Costco is closed. Here is the full breakdown — every major store, service, and institution — so you don't waste a trip.

  • Walmart: ✅ OPEN — regular hours, 6 AM–11 PM
  • Target: ✅ OPEN — regular hours (check local store)
  • Costco: ❌ CLOSED — all warehouses closed
  • Sam's Club: ✅ OPEN — 8 AM–6 PM (Plus members); 10 AM–6 PM (Club members)
  • Aldi: ✅ OPEN — limited hours, check local store
  • Trader Joe's: ✅ OPEN — regular hours
  • Whole Foods: ✅ OPEN — most locations, check locally

  • Walgreens: ✅ OPEN — most locations open
  • Home Depot: ✅ OPEN — regular hours
  • Lowe's: ✅ OPEN — regular hours
  • Best Buy: ✅ OPEN — regular hours
  • Banks: ❌ CLOSED — federal holiday, branches closed (ATMs and online banking available)
  • USPS: ❌ CLOSED — no mail delivery, post offices closed
  • UPS: ✅ OPEN — delivery services continue (some delays possible)

Is Costco open on Memorial Day 2026?

No. Costco is closed on Memorial Day 2026. All Costco warehouse locations nationwide will be closed on May 25. This is Costco's standard policy — the company closes for all major US holidays including Memorial Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day. If you need last-minute cookout supplies, Costco is not an option today. Head to Walmart, Target, or your nearest grocery store instead.

Is Walmart open on Memorial Day 2026?

Yes. Walmart is open on Memorial Day. Most Walmart stores are operating their standard hours of 6 AM to 11 PM in local time zones. A small number of locations may have adjusted hours — it is always worth checking the Walmart app or walmart.com/store-finder before leaving the house, particularly for 24-hour locations which may revert to standard hours today.

Is Target open on Memorial Day 2026?

Yes. Target is open on Memorial Day 2026. Target confirmed all stores will be open during regular hours. Hours vary by location — most Target stores operate 8 AM to 10 PM or 8 AM to 11 PM. Check the Target app or target.com/store-locator for your specific store's hours before heading out.

Is the post office open on Memorial Day? Does USPS deliver?

No. The United States Postal Service observes Memorial Day as one of its 11 federal holidays. Post office locations are closed today, and there is no mail or package delivery on May 25. If you were expecting a package via USPS, it will arrive Tuesday, May 27. This applies to regular mail, Priority Mail, and First Class packages. USPS Express Mail is the one exception — it may still be delivered depending on the specific service level purchased.

Are banks open on Memorial Day 2026?

No. Banks are closed on Memorial Day. Because it is a federal holiday, virtually all bank branches — Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citi, and others — will have their physical locations closed. However, ATMs are fully operational and all online and mobile banking services remain available. The stock market is also closed today — NYSE and NASDAQ resume normal trading Tuesday, May 27.

What is Memorial Day and why do we observe it?

Memorial Day is a federal holiday observed annually on the last Monday of May — in 2026, that is May 25. The holiday honors US military personnel who died in service to the country. Originally known as Decoration Day, the tradition began in the years following the Civil War, when communities decorated the graves of fallen soldiers with flowers. Congress officially designated the last Monday of May as Memorial Day in 1971. The National Moment of Remembrance encourages all Americans to pause at 3:00 PM local time for a moment of silence in honor of those who gave their lives.
