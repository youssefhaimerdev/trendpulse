Walmart Blackstone Parmesan Ranch Recall: What to Check, What to Do, and Why It Matters

Blackstone Products has recalled select lots of Parmesan Ranch seasoning sold at Walmart nationwide over possible Salmonella contamination. The FDA posted the recall on May 15. Here is exactly which products are affected and what to do if you have one.

By TrendPulse Staff·May 27, 2026·3 min read·👁 46,330
Share:

If you bought Blackstone Parmesan Ranch seasoning at Walmart, check your container now. Blackstone Products of Providence, Utah, has voluntarily recalled select lots of its Parmesan Ranch 7.3 oz seasoning due to potential Salmonella contamination from a dry milk powder ingredient. The FDA posted the recall on May 15, 2026. No illnesses have been reported to date, but the FDA recommends you stop using the product immediately and dispose of it or seek a replacement.

Blackstone Parmesan Ranch Recall — What to Check
  • Product: Blackstone Parmesan Ranch Seasoning, 7.3 oz
  • Item number: #4106
  • Where to find lot number: Printed on the BOTTOM of the container
  • Affected lot #1: 2025-43282 — Best By July 2, 2027
  • Affected lot #2: 2025-46172 — Best By August 5, 2027
  • Affected lot #3: 2026-54751 — Best By August 12, 2027
  • Where sold: Walmart stores nationwide + blackstoneproducts.com
  • What to do: Dispose immediately OR call 1-888-879-4610 for a replacement
  • Illnesses reported: None to date

Why was the Blackstone Ranch seasoning recalled?

The recall traces back to a separate, larger recall of dry milk powder by California Dairies, Inc., announced on April 20, 2026. That contaminated dry milk powder — flagged for potential Salmonella — was supplied to a third-party manufacturer and used as an ingredient in the Blackstone Parmesan Ranch seasoning. Once Blackstone Products learned their supplier's ingredient was under recall, they voluntarily initiated their own recall of all products made with that ingredient batch.

Blackstone's Parmesan Ranch is not the only product affected by the California Dairies powder recall. A number of other popular brands — including Ghirardelli chocolate powders, Zapp's potato chips, Dirty potato chips, snack mixes, and popcorn seasonings — have also been recalled as downstream products that used the same contaminated ingredient. The FDA's full list of affected products can be found at fda.gov.

Is your Blackstone seasoning definitely recalled?

Not necessarily. Only specific lot numbers are affected — not all Blackstone Parmesan Ranch products. The lot number is printed on the bottom of the container. If your container shows 2025-43282, 2025-46172, or 2026-54751, it is in the recall. If it shows a different lot number, it is not part of this recall. Check carefully before throwing it away.

What to do if you have a recalled product

The FDA and Blackstone Products recommend three steps. First, stop using the product immediately — do not eat it and do not use it to season food. Second, dispose of it — throw it in the trash, ideally in a sealed bag so animals or other people cannot access it. Third, contact Blackstone Products for a refund or replacement by calling 1-888-879-4610. The company has confirmed it is handling replacement requests through that number.

What are Salmonella symptoms and who is most at risk?

Salmonella is a bacterial infection that typically causes fever, diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, and abdominal cramps within 12 to 72 hours of exposure. Most healthy adults recover within a week without medical treatment. However, the FDA warns that Salmonella can cause serious and sometimes fatal infections in young children, the elderly, pregnant women, and people with weakened immune systems. In rare cases, the infection enters the bloodstream and causes arterial infections, endocarditis, or arthritis. If you believe you or a family member may have consumed this product and are experiencing symptoms, contact your healthcare provider.
