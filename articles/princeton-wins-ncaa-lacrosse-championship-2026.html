Princeton Wins 2026 NCAA Men's Lacrosse Championship: 16–9 Over Notre Dame, First Title Since 2001

No. 1 Princeton dominated No. 2 Notre Dame 16–9 at Scott Stadium to capture their seventh national lacrosse title and first since 2001. Chad Palumbo was unstoppable with his 48th goal of the season. Here is the full story.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 25, 2026·4 min read·👁 22,887
Share:

Princeton won the 2026 NCAA Men's Lacrosse Championship. The No. 1 seed Tigers defeated No. 2 Notre Dame 16–9 at Scott Stadium in Charlottesville, Virginia on Memorial Day Monday, claiming their seventh national title and first since 2001. The result ended Notre Dame's bid for a third consecutive championship, as Princeton's second-quarter explosion — 11 unanswered goals — turned a 3–3 tie into an insurmountable lead the Irish could never overcome.

2026 NCAA Lacrosse Championship — Final
  • Winner: Princeton Tigers (No. 1 seed)
  • Final score: Princeton 16, Notre Dame 9
  • Venue: Scott Stadium, Charlottesville, VA
  • Princeton's title count: 7th national championship
  • Last Princeton title: 2001
  • Chad Palumbo goals: 48th of the season (tournament-high)
  • Princeton tournament record: 4-0
  • Notre Dame record: 13-3 (titles in 2023, 2024)
  • Princeton's run: Defeated Marist, Penn State, Duke, Notre Dame

How the game unfolded: Princeton's 11-goal first half run

Notre Dame came out with purpose and seized a shocking 3–0 lead inside the first six minutes. Will Maheras opened the scoring two minutes in, followed quickly by Josh Yago (his 33rd goal of the season) and Matt Jeffery 23 seconds later. The early signs suggested this might be the competitive final the pre-game preview had promised.

Then Princeton turned it on. Chad Palumbo got his first goal at the 8:31 mark of the first quarter, and the Tigers never truly looked back. After Porter Malkiel tied it at 3–3, Princeton went on a jaw-dropping 11-goal unanswered run — straddling the first and second quarters — to lead 11–3 at the half. Palumbo was the catalyst, finishing with his 48th goal of the season on a wraparound with 22 seconds left before intermission. It was his fifth game this season with four or more goals.

"We weren't going to be denied. This group put in too much work all year. We came here to finish it." — Princeton head coach Matt Madalon

Chad Palumbo's historic season

No player defined Princeton's dominant run more than senior attackman Chad Palumbo. His 48 goals on the season established a new Princeton program record, and his performance across four tournament games — including a five-goal effort in the semifinal against Duke — was the backbone of the Tigers' national title run. Palumbo is expected to be a top selection in the upcoming PLL college draft.

Notre Dame falls short of three-peat

For Notre Dame, the loss was brutal in its timing. The Irish had won the national championship in 2023 and 2024 and entered this game as the only program with a realistic shot at three consecutive titles in the modern era. Junior Josh Yago's 33 goals during the regular season had made him one of the most electric players in college lacrosse, and the Irish had beaten No. 6 Syracuse convincingly in the semifinal. But Princeton's defensive scheme neutralized Notre Dame's transition attack in the second quarter, and the Irish never recovered from the first-half deficit. Luke Miller's man-up goal in the third briefly gave fans hope at 12–7, but Princeton's Tucker Wade answered with his 13th multi-goal game of the season to extend the lead.

Princeton's place in lacrosse history

With seven national championships, Princeton now ranks among the most decorated programs in NCAA men's lacrosse history. Their first title was in 1992, the beginning of a decade-long dynasty that produced six championships through 2001. The 25-year gap between their sixth and seventh titles is the longest in program history — but the 2026 team ended it emphatically, going undefeated in the tournament and never trailing after the first quarter of the championship game.
