Josh Jacobs Arrested: Green Bay Packers RB Faces Felony Strangulation and Four Other Charges

Green Bay Packers running back Josh Jacobs was arrested Tuesday and booked into Brown County Jail on five charges, including felony strangulation and suffocation. The incident stems from a May 23 disturbance call. Jacobs vehemently denies the allegations through his attorneys.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 27, 2026·4 min read·👁 51,882
Share:

Josh Jacobs has been arrested. The Green Bay Packers running back was booked into Brown County Jail on Tuesday, May 26, 2026, on five charges stemming from a domestic disturbance call on May 23. The most serious charge is felony strangulation and suffocation. Jacobs, 28, faces a court appearance Wednesday afternoon. Through his attorneys, he vehemently denies all allegations. The Packers acknowledged the arrest and said they will withhold further comment.

Josh Jacobs Arrest — Key Facts
  • Arrested: Tuesday, May 26, 2026
  • Booked: Brown County Jail, Wisconsin
  • Charges: Felony strangulation and suffocation · Battery/domestic abuse · Criminal damage to property/domestic abuse · Disorderly conduct/domestic abuse · Intimidation of a victim
  • Original incident: May 23, 2026 at 8:37 AM — disturbance complaint in Hobart-Lawrence, WI

  • Packers statement: "Aware of the matter...ongoing legal situation...no further comment"

What happened? The full story of Josh Jacobs' arrest

On Saturday morning, May 23, at approximately 8:37 AM, the Hobart-Lawrence Police Department in Wisconsin was dispatched to a disturbance complaint involving Jacobs at a residence in the Hobart-Lawrence area — the Green Bay suburb where the running back lives during the season. Officers responded and investigated the incident. Three days later, on Tuesday May 26, as a result of that investigation, Jacobs was formally arrested and booked into Brown County Jail.

The Hobart-Lawrence Police Department confirmed the arrest in a press release and stated that the investigation remains active and ongoing. No additional details about the specific circumstances of the incident were released. Jacobs is expected to make his initial court appearance Wednesday afternoon.

What are the charges against Josh Jacobs?

Jacobs faces five charges. The most serious is strangulation and suffocation — a felony in Wisconsin. The remaining four are misdemeanors, all carrying domestic abuse modifiers: battery, criminal damage to property, disorderly conduct, and intimidation of a victim. In Wisconsin, domestic abuse modifiers apply when the alleged victim has or has had a relationship with the accused — they increase the potential penalties at sentencing and are treated more seriously by prosecutors and courts than equivalent non-domestic charges.

A felony strangulation charge in Wisconsin carries a maximum of 6 years in prison and $10,000 in fines upon conviction. Jacobs' legal team — David Chesnoff and Richard Schonfeld, prominent Las Vegas-based criminal defense attorneys — issued a firm statement: "Josh vehemently denies the allegations, and this matter is in the early stages of investigation with important evidence that has not yet been made public. We ask for fairness and restraint."

What does this mean for the Packers?

The timing is significant. Jacobs is entering his third season with Green Bay and is the unquestioned anchor of the Packers' running game. He is under contract through 2027 on a deal that pays him $11.5 million in 2026 and $13.5 million in 2027, though no guarantees remain on the contract — meaning Green Bay could cut him without a significant cap penalty (approximately $6.25 million in dead money spread across two years). His 2025 season was slightly down from his Pro Bowl 2024 year: 929 rushing yards and 13 touchdowns in 16 games.

Beyond the contractual picture, the NFL's Personal Conduct Policy gives Commissioner Roger Goodell the authority to suspend players for conduct detrimental to the league regardless of the outcome of criminal proceedings. Given the severity of the felony charge and the domestic abuse components, a suspension investigation by the NFL is likely. Green Bay faces immediate pressure to decide how to handle Jacobs' roster status as the legal process develops.

Josh Jacobs' prior legal history

This is not Jacobs' first encounter with law enforcement. In January 2021, he was involved in a single-car accident in Las Vegas and was briefly detained on suspicion of DUI. He was released after receiving treatment for injuries sustained in the crash. He ultimately faced only a minor traffic charge — failure to decrease speed — and the case was closed after he paid a fine. Tuesday's arrest, involving a felony charge with domestic abuse components, represents a substantially more serious situation.
