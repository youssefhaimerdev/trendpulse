Champions League Final 2026: Arsenal vs PSG — How to Watch, Start Time, Preview and What's at Stake

The biggest club match in world football is today. Arsenal face defending champions PSG in the 2026 UEFA Champions League Final at Puskás Aréna in Budapest at 12 PM ET. Here is everything you need to watch it and understand it.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 30, 2026·5 min read·👁 87,334
Share:

The 2026 UEFA Champions League Final kicks off today at 12:00 PM ET. Arsenal take on defending champions Paris Saint-Germain at the Puskás Aréna in Budapest, Hungary. It is the biggest club football match in the world — and for Arsenal, it is the culmination of years of rebuilding under manager Mikel Arteta. For PSG, it is a chance to become back-to-back European champions for the first time in the club's history. The Killers will headline the pre-match concert at the stadium.

Champions League Final 2026 — Quick Facts
  • Teams: PSG (defending champions) vs Arsenal
  • Date: Saturday, May 30, 2026
  • Kickoff: 12:00 PM ET / 6:00 PM local (Budapest)
  • Venue: Puskás Aréna, Budapest, Hungary
  • US broadcast: CBS and Paramount+
  • Streaming: Paramount+, Fubo, YouTube TV (with CBS)
  • Kickoff show: The Killers perform live
  • Arsenal's last UCL Final: 2006 (lost to Barcelona)
  • PSG's last UCL title: 2025 (beat Inter Milan)
  • Arsenal's recent form: Won Premier League title last week — 22-year drought ended

Where to watch the Champions League Final in the US

The match airs in the US on CBS and Paramount+. If you have cable, find CBS on your local channel. For streaming, Paramount+ with the Essentials plan ($7.99/month) includes CBS and all UEFA coverage. You can also watch through Fubo (includes CBS Sports and Paramount+), YouTube TV (CBS included), Hulu + Live TV, and DirecTV Stream. TUDN will broadcast Spanish-language coverage for Spanish-speaking viewers in the US.

Arsenal vs PSG — what to expect

Arsenal enter as the slight favorites, riding the momentum of winning their first Premier League title in 22 years just seven days ago. Arteta's side has been the most consistent team in Europe this season — organized in defense, dynamic in transition, and led by a front line that has rediscovered its ruthlessness. Bukayo Saka, Leandro Trossard, and Kai Havertz have been the standout attackers across the tournament. Declan Rice scored twice from set pieces in the semifinal win over Real Madrid, making him one of the most talked-about players heading into the final.

PSG are not here by accident. Under Luis Enrique, the Parisians have evolved from a star-driven collection of individuals into a genuine team — tactically disciplined, high-pressing, and dangerous on the counter. They beat Arsenal in last year's semifinal before going on to claim the title against Inter Milan. Ousmane Dembélé and Bradley Barcola have been exceptional, and goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma has been arguably the best shot-stopper in this year's competition.

"We've worked so hard to get here. Now we need to be brave enough to go and win it." — Mikel Arteta, press conference Friday

Head to head and history

Arsenal and PSG have played six times this season across the league phase and knockout rounds. Arsenal won the league-phase meeting 2-0 in October, while PSG won the reverse fixture 1-0 in Paris. In the semifinal, Arsenal won the first leg 2-1 at the Emirates and held PSG to a goalless draw in Paris. This is the first time the two clubs have met in a Champions League final — and the first UCL final to feature a British club as favorite in years.

Will PSG win back-to-back Champions League titles?

Only three clubs in the competition's history have won back-to-back titles in the Champions League era — Real Madrid (multiple times), AC Milan in 1989-90, and Bayern Munich in 2013. PSG won it last year for the first time in club history. Repeating would be extraordinary. Their experience of having been through a final — and won it — may prove the decisive psychological edge. Arsenal have never won this trophy. That pressure is real.

When does the Champions League Final start and end?

Kickoff is at 12:00 PM ET. A standard match is 90 minutes plus stoppage time. If level after 90 minutes, the game goes to 30 minutes of extra time, then potentially a penalty shootout. Total broadcast is expected to run until approximately 3:00-3:30 PM ET including pre-match and post-match coverage.
