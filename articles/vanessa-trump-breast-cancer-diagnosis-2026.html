Vanessa Trump Reveals Breast Cancer Diagnosis: What She Said, Tiger Woods' Response, and Kai Trump's Tribute

Vanessa Trump, Tiger Woods' girlfriend and the ex-wife of Donald Trump Jr., announced Wednesday that she has been diagnosed with breast cancer and has already undergone a procedure. Here is everything she shared and the outpouring of support that followed.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 21, 2026·4 min read·👁 28,441
Share:

Vanessa Trump has breast cancer. The 48-year-old socialite, former model, and mother of five announced her diagnosis in an emotional Instagram post on Wednesday, May 20, 2026 — just one week after her boyfriend Tiger Woods completed a stay in rehabilitation. Trump confirmed she has already undergone a medical procedure and is now working with specialists on a treatment plan. She asked for privacy for herself and her children during the process.

Vanessa Trump — Key Facts
  • Age: 48 (born December 18, 1977)
  • Diagnosis: Breast cancer — announced May 20, 2026
  • Status: Underwent a procedure; working with medical team on treatment plan
  • Relationship: Dating Tiger Woods since 2024; relationship public since March 2025
  • Ex-husband: Donald Trump Jr. (divorced 2018)
  • Children with Don Jr.: Kai (18), Donald III (17), Tristan (14), Spencer (13), Chloe (11)
  • Kai Trump: Attending University of Miami for golf — September 2026

What did Vanessa Trump say in her announcement?

Trump's Instagram post was measured and graceful. "While this isn't the news anyone expects," she wrote, "I'm working closely with my medical team for a treatment plan and staying focused and hopeful while surrounded by the love and support of my family, my kids, and those closest to me." She thanked healthcare workers for their role in her care and asked that her family's privacy be respected as they navigate the process together.

The post did not specify what type of breast cancer she has been diagnosed with or what stage, which is consistent with how many public figures handle health announcements — sharing enough to inform without disclosing clinical details that belong to her medical team and family.

How did Kai Trump and the Trump family respond?

Reaction from the Trump family was swift and public. Vanessa's oldest daughter Kai Trump — the 18-year-old golfer who has become a public figure in her own right — posted a photo of herself and her mother smiling together with the caption: "Strongest person I know. Love you." The post drew hundreds of thousands of interactions within hours.

Ivanka Trump commented directly on Vanessa's post: "Praying for your continued strength and a swift recovery. Love you mama." Tiffany Trump also reacted publicly. The show of family solidarity was widely noted, given that Vanessa's divorce from Donald Trump Jr. was finalized in 2018 after 12 years of marriage.

"Strongest person I know. Love you." — Kai Trump on Instagram, responding to her mother's announcement

Who is Vanessa Trump and how did she meet Tiger Woods?

Vanessa Haydon Trump grew up in New York and worked as a model and actress before marrying Donald Trump Jr. in 2005. After their divorce, she maintained a relatively low public profile until her relationship with Tiger Woods became public in March 2025 — when Woods announced it himself on Instagram. The couple has since appeared together at multiple golf events, including TGL matches in Florida, where Vanessa and Kai have cheered Woods from the stands.

The relationship has attracted significant attention given both individuals' connections to the Trump family and Woods' own turbulent period — which included multiple surgeries, a DUI arrest in March 2026 after he rolled his Range Rover near Jupiter Island, Florida, and a subsequent rehabilitation stay. Vanessa publicly stood by him throughout. "Vanessa and Tiger are in love, still serious, and happy," a source close to the couple told multiple outlets following the DUI incident.

What is the outlook for breast cancer patients?

Breast cancer survival rates have improved significantly over recent decades. According to the American Cancer Society, the five-year survival rate for localized breast cancer — cancer that has not spread beyond the breast — is approximately 99%. For regional cancer (spread to nearby lymph nodes), the rate is around 86%. The overall outlook depends on the specific type, stage, and individual factors, all of which Vanessa has not publicly disclosed. The trend in treatment outcomes, however, is consistently positive, driven by early detection and ongoing advances in targeted therapies.
