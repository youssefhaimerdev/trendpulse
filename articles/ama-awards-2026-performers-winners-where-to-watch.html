AMA Awards 2026: Start Time, Channel, Performers, Host and Where to Watch Tonight

The 2026 American Music Awards are happening tonight. Nikki Glaser is hosting. Here is every detail you need — what channel, what time, who's performing, and how to stream it if you don't have cable.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 25, 2026·3 min read·👁 19,554
Share:

The 2026 American Music Awards air tonight, Memorial Day Monday, May 25. The ceremony is being held in Los Angeles and broadcast on CBS, with comedian Nikki Glaser serving as host. Coverage begins at 8:00 PM ET / 5:00 PM PT. Here is everything you need to know before it starts.

AMA Awards 2026 — At a Glance
  • Date: Monday, May 25, 2026
  • Start time: 8:00 PM ET / 5:00 PM PT
  • Channel: CBS
  • Stream: Paramount+ (with CBS subscription), CBS.com
  • Host: Nikki Glaser
  • Location: Los Angeles, CA
  • Duration: Approximately 3 hours

What channel are the AMA Awards on tonight?

The 2026 AMAs are airing on CBS. Check your local listings for the exact channel number — CBS is available on all major cable and satellite providers. The show begins at 8:00 PM Eastern Time and is expected to run approximately three hours.

How to watch the AMAs 2026 without cable

You have several options to stream tonight's AMA Awards without a traditional cable subscription:

Paramount+ — The show streams live on Paramount+ for subscribers who have access to the CBS live feed. The Essential plan ($7.99/month) includes CBS in most markets. CBS.com / CBS App — You can stream the AMAs live on CBS's own website and app with a free account, depending on your local market. YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, DirecTV Stream, FuboTV — All four include CBS in their channel packages and carry live CBS programming including tonight's AMAs.

Who is hosting the AMA Awards 2026?

Comedian and television host Nikki Glaser is hosting the 2026 AMAs. Glaser, who became one of the hottest names in entertainment after her widely praised Golden Globes hosting performance, brings her sharp, self-aware comedic style to music's AMAs for the first time. Her booking generated immediate excitement from both the comedy and music fandoms.

Who is performing at the AMA Awards 2026?

The full performers list has not been entirely confirmed ahead of broadcast, but CBS has announced a lineup that spans country, pop, hip-hop, and R&B. Confirmed performers include acts from across the 2026 AMA nominees list. CBS has teased "surprise guest appearances" and a tribute segment expected to be one of the night's most talked-about moments. Check CBS's official social channels and CBS.com for any last-minute performance announcements before the 8 PM start.

Who are the major nominees?

The American Music Awards nominations are determined by a combination of streaming data, sales, airplay, and touring statistics from the past eligibility year. The 2026 cycle covers the period from April 2025 through March 2026. Sabrina Carpenter, Morgan Wallen, Kendrick Lamar, Chappell Roan, and Billie Eilish lead the major categories going into tonight's show. Daily Pulse will update this article with the full winners list as the ceremony progresses — check back during and after the show for live results.
