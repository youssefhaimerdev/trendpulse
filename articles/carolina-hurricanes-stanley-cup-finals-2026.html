Hurricanes Crush Canadiens 6-1 in Game 5 to Reach Stanley Cup Final — Carolina Faces Golden Knights Starting Tuesday

The Carolina Hurricanes are going to the Stanley Cup Final for the first time since 2006. Their 6-1 demolition of the Montreal Canadiens in Game 5 was complete and dominant. Game 1 against the Vegas Golden Knights is Tuesday on ABC.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 30, 2026·4 min read·👁 54,882
Share:

The Carolina Hurricanes are in the Stanley Cup Final. Carolina crushed the Montreal Canadiens 6-1 in Game 5 on Friday night at Lenovo Center in Raleigh to win the Eastern Conference Final 4-1. The Hurricanes advance to the Stanley Cup Final for the first time since 2006 — the year they won the Cup — and will face the Vegas Golden Knights starting Tuesday night on ABC. Carolina enters the Final with a 12-1 playoff record, the best in the league.

Hurricanes vs Canadiens ECF — Series Summary
  • Series result: Hurricanes win 4-1
  • Game 5 score: Carolina 6, Montreal 1 (May 29)
  • Carolina's playoff record: 12-1
  • Goals allowed in 13 games: 21 (1.6 per game)
  • Goalie: Frederik Andersen — frontrunner for Conn Smythe Trophy
  • Key scorers: Nikolaj Ehlers, Andrei Svechnikov, Seth Jarvis, Taylor Hall
  • Stanley Cup Final: vs Vegas Golden Knights
  • Game 1: Tuesday, June 2 — time TBD — on ABC
  • Last Carolina Cup appearance: 2006 (won)

How Game 5 unfolded — Carolina's statement night

After two close, tension-filled overtime games in the series, Game 5 was never a contest. The Hurricanes came out with an intensity that Montreal simply had no answer for. Carolina scored three goals in the first period and never looked back. The crowd at Lenovo Center was deafening from the opening faceoff — and the team matched their energy.

Frederik Andersen, the 36-year-old Danish goaltender who has been the backbone of Carolina's run, stopped 23 of 24 shots. His performance was particularly meaningful given the circumstances: his agent and close friend, Claude Lemieux, had died by suicide the night before Game 5. Andersen played through grief, and his teammates rallied around him. "He's our backbone," forward Taylor Hall said after the game. "Tonight was special. He gave us everything."

"Carolina is a machine. We couldn't find an answer. They deserved every bit of it." — Montreal coach Martin St. Louis

Carolina's historic playoff run

The Hurricanes' 12-1 playoff record is extraordinary. To put it in context: only six teams in NHL history have entered the Stanley Cup Final with a better record through this point. Carolina swept the Ottawa Senators (4-0) in the first round, then swept the Philadelphia Flyers (4-0) in the second. They won four of five against Montreal. They have not lost two games in a row at any point in the postseason.

The defensive numbers are equally remarkable. Carolina has allowed just 21 goals in 13 games — 1.6 per game. In the modern NHL, that is almost unheard of. Andersen's save percentage in the playoffs is .944. Head coach Rod Brind'Amour has constructed a system so suffocating that teams simply run out of options against it. The Golden Knights, who swept the Avalanche, will be the best test yet.

Carolina vs Vegas Golden Knights — Stanley Cup Final preview

This is a matchup between the two best defensive teams in the 2026 playoffs. The Golden Knights allowed just 21 goals in their 11 wins — matching Carolina's pace. Carter Hart in goal for Vegas has been brilliant, posting a .938 save percentage. For Carolina, Mark Stone's health will be a factor — the VGK captain returned from injury during the WCF and looked strong in Game 4.

Vegas has the edge in playoff experience, having reached the Cup Final twice before. Carolina has the home ice advantage — if the series opens in Raleigh, the atmosphere at Lenovo Center will be as electric as anything in the sport.

When and where to watch — Stanley Cup Final 2026 schedule

Game 1 of the Stanley Cup Final between the Carolina Hurricanes and Vegas Golden Knights is scheduled for Tuesday, June 2. The game will air on ABC and stream on ESPN+. Subsequent games alternate between Raleigh and Las Vegas. Game 2 is Thursday, June 4. Games 3 and 4 are in Las Vegas. Full series schedule is available at nhl.com/stanleycup.
