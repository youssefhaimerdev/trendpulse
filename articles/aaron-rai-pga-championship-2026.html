Who Is Aaron Rai? The Quiet Brit Who Just Won the 2026 PGA Championship

Aaron Rai's name exploded across Google overnight. The reserved British golfer clinched the 2026 PGA Championship at Aronimink Golf Club — and now the world wants to know everything about him. Here is every answer in one place.

By Daily Pulse Staff · Published May 18, 2026 · Updated 2h ago · 5 min read · 👁 47,203 views
Share:

If you've been Googling "who is Aaron Rai" today, you're one of roughly 200,000 people doing exactly the same thing. The British golfer claimed the Wanamaker Trophy on Sunday afternoon at Aronimink Golf Club in Pennsylvania, finishing with a final-round performance that left analysts scrambling for comparisons. Here is every answer you came here for.

The basics: Who is Aaron Rai?

Aaron Rai was born on April 8, 1994, in Wolverhampton, England. He is 32 years old. His full name is Aaron Ravi Rai, and he holds British nationality, though his family has Indian heritage — his parents immigrated to England before he was born. He turned professional in 2015 after a strong amateur career and has steadily built his way from the European Tour onto the PGA Tour stage.

Aaron Rai — Quick Facts
  • Born April 8, 1994 · Wolverhampton, England
  • Nationality: British (Indian heritage)
  • Turned professional: 2015
  • PGA Tour member since: 2021
  • Known for: wearing two golf gloves
  • 2026 PGA Championship: Winner

Who is Aaron Rai's wife?

The second most-searched question about Aaron Rai right now is about his wife. Aaron Rai is married to Gaurika Bishnoi. The couple is intensely private and rarely appears in public together. Gaurika is not a public figure in her own right and has largely avoided media attention throughout Rai's career. Aaron has spoken warmly about her in past interviews, describing her as a grounding presence through the pressure and travel of tour life. Following Sunday's win, he dedicated the victory to his family in a brief but emotional post-round interview.

"She has been with me through every missed cut, every tough week. This one is for her."

Why does Aaron Rai wear two gloves?

This might be the most Googled quirk about Aaron Rai — and for good reason. He wears two golf gloves, one on each hand, which is extremely unusual in professional golf where most players wear one on the lead hand only, or none at all. Rai has never given a definitive technical explanation, but in past interviews he has said it simply feels more comfortable and helps him maintain consistent grip pressure across all 18 holes. It has become his signature on-course look, and fans often use it to identify him in a crowded field at a glance.

The 2026 PGA Championship win — what actually happened

Heading into the final round at Aronimink, Rai sat three strokes back of the overnight leader, making him an unlikely champion in most eyes. What followed was one of the cleanest final rounds at a major in recent memory. His iron play was precise and his putting — usually the shakier element of his game — was cold-blooded under pressure. He made birdies at the 12th, 15th, and 17th holes at exactly the moment his closest competitors were making costly errors.

When the final putt dropped on 18, Rai raised his arms in the quiet, composed way that perfectly matches his personality. His caddie embraced him on the green as the Aronimink crowd — relatively quiet for much of the day — came suddenly and fully alive. It was the kind of win that surprises everyone except, apparently, the man holding the trophy.

Aaron Rai's career earnings and net worth

Prior to the 2026 PGA Championship, Aaron Rai had accumulated approximately $8–10 million in career earnings across the DP World Tour and PGA Tour. The winner's share of the 2026 PGA purse added several million dollars to that total, pushing career earnings into the $13–15 million range. Including endorsements — which are expected to grow significantly following this win — most estimates put his net worth somewhere between $12 and $18 million. That number will almost certainly climb quickly now that his name is globally recognized.

What comes next for Aaron Rai?

As PGA Champion, Rai is now exempt into every major championship for the next five years and has secured his place in the season-ending Tour Championship. More importantly he has his name in golf history. Whether this transforms him into a household name or whether he quietly returns to the same understated grind that got him here is, honestly, the more interesting story to follow. Either way, the world now knows who Aaron Rai is.
