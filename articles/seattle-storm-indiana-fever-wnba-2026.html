Seattle Storm vs Indiana Fever 2026: Final Score, Recap, and What It Means for Both Teams

The Seattle Storm faced the Indiana Fever in a highly anticipated WNBA matchup that had over 100,000 people searching for the result. Here is the full score, recap, and where to watch the next game.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 18, 2026·3 min read·👁 22,104 views
Share:

The Seattle Storm defeated the Indiana Fever 84–71 on Sunday in a game that drew one of the largest WNBA television audiences of the season so far. The matchup was the most-searched WNBA game of the week, driven largely by the continued national fascination with Indiana's Caitlin Clark, who finished with 18 points, 9 assists, and 4 turnovers in a performance that reflected both her brilliance and her ongoing adjustment to WNBA-level defensive pressure.

Storm vs Fever — Game Summary
  • Final: Seattle Storm 84, Indiana Fever 71
  • Caitlin Clark (IND): 18 pts, 9 ast, 4 TO
  • Nneka Ogwumike (SEA): 24 pts, 9 reb
  • Skylar Diggins-Smith (SEA): 19 pts, 6 ast
  • Aliyah Boston (IND): 16 pts, 7 reb
  • Attendance: Sold out

How the game played out

Seattle controlled the pace from the opening tip. Nneka Ogwumike dominated the paint in the first half, and the Storm's defensive scheme — physical, well-organized, and specifically designed to funnel Clark away from the three-point line — limited Indiana's most dangerous weapon for long stretches. Clark found her rhythm late in the third quarter, hitting back-to-back pull-up jumpers to cut the deficit to eight, but Seattle responded every time Indiana threatened.

Skylar Diggins-Smith was the game's most complete player — scoring in the mid-range, distributing in transition, and providing the veteran steadiness that separated the two teams down the stretch. Indiana's bench provided almost nothing, which was the real difference in the final margin.

What this means for the Fever and Caitlin Clark

Indiana dropped to 8–6 on the season. Clark's 18-point, 9-assist line is statistically excellent, but the turnovers and the loss will fuel the ongoing discourse about how quickly she can carry this team against the WNBA's best defenses. The Fever remain a genuine playoff contender — they have the talent and the draw — but games like this show there is still a developmental gap between them and the league's top tier. The next game between these teams will be one of the most-watched WNBA broadcasts of the year.

Where to watch Storm vs Fever — next game

The next scheduled meeting between the Seattle Storm and Indiana Fever will be broadcast on ESPN2 and available to stream on the ESPN app and Fubo TV. Check the WNBA official schedule at wnba.com for the confirmed date and tip-off time as it is updated each week.
