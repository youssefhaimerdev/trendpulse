ACM Awards 2026: Full Winners List, Performances, and the Moments Everyone Is Talking About

The 2026 Academy of Country Music Awards delivered a night of surprises, standout performances, and emotional moments. Here is every winner, every performer, and everything that happened.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 18, 2026·4 min read·👁 38,901 views
Share:

The 2026 Academy of Country Music Awards took place Sunday night, delivering country music's biggest night with a mix of expected champions and genuine surprises. The ceremony, broadcast live, drew massive viewership and sent searches for winner names surging across Google. Here is the complete rundown of every award and the moments that defined the evening.

ACM Awards 2026 — Key Winners
  • Entertainer of the Year: Morgan Wallen
  • Female Vocalist of the Year: Lainey Wilson
  • Male Vocalist of the Year: Morgan Wallen
  • Album of the Year: Lainey Wilson — Whirlwind
  • Song of the Year: "White Horse" — Chris Stapleton
  • Single of the Year: "Fast Car" — Luke Combs
  • New Female Artist: Megan Moroney
  • New Male Artist: Zach Bryan (fan vote)
  • Duo/Group of the Year: Brothers Osborne

Entertainer of the Year: Morgan Wallen wins again

Morgan Wallen claimed the night's top honor for the third consecutive time, solidifying his position as the dominant commercial force in country music. His acceptance speech was notably more subdued than prior years — short, grateful, and focused on his band and crew. The win was widely expected but still drew loud applause from the arena crowd.

Lainey Wilson's dominant night

Lainey Wilson was the story of the evening. Her album Whirlwind claimed Album of the Year and she added Female Vocalist to her trophy case. Wilson's performance of her latest single was a highlight of the broadcast — a full-production set piece that had the arena on its feet by the second chorus. She is now the most-awarded female artist at the ACMs over the past three years combined.

Best moments of the night

The broadcast's most emotional moment came from Chris Stapleton, who accepted the Song of the Year award for "White Horse" and dedicated it to his late collaborator. The standing ovation lasted nearly two minutes. On the lighter side, a surprise duet between Post Malone and Luke Combs — both in full Western attire — brought the house down and generated massive social media chatter before the show had even ended.

Complete winners list

Beyond the headline categories, Megan Moroney won New Female Artist in an upset over the heavily-favored Nate Smith and Tyler Hubbard. Brothers Osborne claimed Duo/Group of the Year for a second straight year. The Crystal Milestone Award went to Alan Jackson, who received a lengthy standing tribute video before appearing briefly onstage to accept. Full list of all 23 categories is available on the official ACM Awards website.
