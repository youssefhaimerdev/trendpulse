PGA Championship 2026 Prize Money: How Much Did Aaron Rai Win and the Full Payout for Every Golfer

The 2026 PGA Championship at Aronimink Golf Club came with a record $20 million purse. Aaron Rai took home $3.6 million for the victory. Here is the complete breakdown of what every golfer earned.

By Daily Pulse Staff·May 18, 2026·4 min read·👁 19,560 views
Share:

The 2026 PGA Championship carried a total purse of $20 million, making it the richest PGA Championship in history. Winner Aaron Rai collected $3.6 million — the largest single check of his career by a significant margin and enough to push his lifetime PGA Tour earnings past $13 million. Here is how the money was distributed among all players who made the cut at Aronimink Golf Club.

2026 PGA Championship — Top 10 Payouts
  • 1st — Aaron Rai: $3,600,000
  • 2nd: $2,160,000
  • 3rd: $1,360,000
  • 4th: $960,000
  • 5th: $800,000
  • 6th: $720,000
  • 7th: $670,000
  • 8th: $620,000
  • 9th: $580,000
  • 10th: $540,000
  • Total purse: $20,000,000
  • Made cut (approx.): 70 players, all paid

How PGA Championship prize money is calculated

The PGA Championship uses a tiered payout structure where the winner receives 18% of the total purse. Each subsequent finishing position receives a smaller percentage according to the official PGA of America payout table. All players who make the cut receive a minimum payment — in 2026, the minimum payout for making the cut at the PGA Championship was approximately $12,500. Players who miss the cut receive nothing.

How does this compare to other majors?

The 2026 PGA Championship's $20 million purse trails only The Players Championship ($25 million) among major professional golf events. The Masters paid $18 million in 2026. The US Open and The Open Championship are expected to announce increased purses later this season. Golf's prize money has increased dramatically over the past decade, driven by competition from LIV Golf and broader sports media rights growth.

What Aaron Rai's winnings mean for his career

Beyond the immediate $3.6 million payday, the PGA Championship victory earns Rai exempt status in all four major championships for the next five years and a spot in the year-end Tour Championship. His world ranking will jump significantly following the win, and endorsement income — which was modest before Sunday — is expected to increase substantially as his profile rises globally. For a player who was largely unknown outside hardcore golf circles before this week, the financial and professional implications of the win are transformative.
