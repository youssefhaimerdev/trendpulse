1. Introduction

Welcome to TrendPulse ("we", "us", "our"). We operate the website located at trendpulse-three-blond.vercel.app (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Site. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please stop using the Site.

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.

2. Information We Collect

Information collected automatically

When you visit our Site, certain information is automatically collected by our analytics and advertising partners. This may include:

  • Your IP address (anonymized where required by law)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages you visit on our Site and the time spent on each page
  • The webpage that referred you to our Site
  • The date and time of your visit
  • Clickstream data (which links you click)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • General geographic location (country, region — not precise location)

Information you provide voluntarily

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. We do not collect your name, phone number, address, or any other personal identifiers unless you contact us directly via email.

Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, health data, or any sensitive personal categories. We do not require account creation to read our content.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to operate our Site and improve your experience. The cookies used on TrendPulse fall into the following categories:

Essential cookies

Required for the Site to function. These cannot be disabled. They do not track personal information.

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with our Site. Google Analytics places cookies on your device to collect anonymized usage data. This data helps us improve our content and understand which stories our readers find most valuable. Google Analytics data is processed by Google LLC in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

We display advertisements on our Site through Google AdSense. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Site and other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our Site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our Site. For more information on managing cookies, visit allaboutcookies.org.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through our Site for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site and its content
  • To analyze usage patterns and understand reader preferences
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • To send newsletters to subscribers who have opted in (email address only)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To respond to inquiries and support requests

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

Service providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers that assist us in operating our Site, including:

  • Google LLC — Analytics (Google Analytics 4) and advertising (Google AdSense). Google Privacy Policy →
  • Vercel Inc. — Website hosting and deployment. Vercel Privacy Policy →

Legal requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

Business transfers

If TrendPulse is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change in ownership and your choices regarding your information.

6. Third-Party Advertising

TrendPulse participates in the Google AdSense program. As a result, Google and its advertising partners may display advertisements on our Site that are based on your browsing history and interests. These advertisers may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites.

We do not control the cookies or tracking technologies used by our advertising partners. Their use of your information is governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party advertisers whose ads appear on our Site.

To opt out of interest-based advertising from Google, visit Google Ads Settings or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

7. Data Retention

We retain analytics data for a maximum of 26 months as configured in our Google Analytics property. Newsletter subscriber email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe. We do not retain other personal information beyond what is necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right to access — Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Right to correction — Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Right to deletion — Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to opt out — Opt out of analytics tracking and personalized advertising
  • Right to data portability — Request a portable copy of your data (where applicable)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@trendpulse.news. We will respond to all verifiable requests within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

TrendPulse is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@trendpulse.news and we will take steps to delete such information.

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) provides you with specific rights. You have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete that information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a CCPA request, email privacy@trendpulse.news.
